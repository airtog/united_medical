The Medical Examination for Your Green Card Filing

The green card medical exam is the USCIS I-693 immigration medical examination. It must be completed by a USCIS-designated civil surgeon, then sealed for submission with your immigration filing or in response to a USCIS request.

Medical Examination for Green Card Applicants

People search for green card medical exam, medical examination for green card, green card physical exam, and green card immigration medical exam. These all refer to the same practical requirement: USCIS needs a completed Form I-693 from a designated civil surgeon.

What Happens at the Visit

The visit usually includes identity review, vaccination record review, a physical examination, blood draw for required testing, urine testing when required, and tuberculosis screening. Dr. Weiss completes the civil surgeon portion of the form. The sealed packet is prepared after the required results are ready.
