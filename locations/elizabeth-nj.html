What to Bring With You

How Long Your Sealed I-693 Stays Valid

This is the question we are asked most, and the answer is not what most websites still say. A Form I-693 signed on or after November 1, 2023 does not expire on a calendar date the way older forms did. Since June 11, 2025, however, USCIS treats the form as valid only for the application it accompanied — it cannot be reused for a different or future filing, and a denied or withdrawn case means starting the medical exam over. Forms signed before November 1, 2023 still follow the previous two-year rule. If you are applying from Elizabeth, time your exam to your filing rather than getting ahead of it.

Accuracy Is the Whole Point

Elizabeth applicants travel a little further to reach us, and they do it for a specific reason. The paperwork is where immigration medical exams go wrong. A physician who is not USCIS-designated invalidates the exam outright. Beyond that, the recurring failures are mundane and entirely avoidable: an edition of the form USCIS no longer accepts, an unsigned or inconsistently dated document, an incomplete TB classification, a vaccination section left blank, a missing age-required test, a positive tuberculosis screen filed without its chest x-ray, or a broken seal on the envelope. Every one of them produces a Request for Evidence and pushes a case back by months — and since June 2025, because the form is valid only for the application it accompanied, a failed filing means a completely new exam rather than a reusable one. Elizabeth applicants are not buying thirty minutes of a doctor's time; they are buying a document that clears on the first submission.

It is the same thing immigration attorneys care about. The exam is one of the few components of a filing an attorney cannot control directly, so they refer to practices whose forms come back correct — complete, internally consistent, on the current edition, and sealed properly. Dr. Weiss brings more than 40 years of practice to that, and immigration exams are the core work of this office. We are glad to coordinate directly with your attorney's office on timing or delivery.
