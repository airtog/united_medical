Receive Your Sealed I-693

Lab results are ready in 5–7 business days. Once complete, we seal your I-693 in a USCIS-compliant envelope and notify you for pickup or mailing. A form signed on or after November 1, 2023 has no calendar expiration date, but under USCIS policy effective June 11, 2025 it is valid only for the application it is filed with — so it is best completed in step with your filing.
