Complete Form I-693 With a USCIS Civil Surgeon

Form I-693 is the Report of Immigration Medical Examination and Vaccination Record. USCIS requires it for many green card and adjustment of status filings, and it must be completed by a USCIS-designated civil surgeon. United Medical Immigration Exams completes I-693 medical exams at 842 Clifton Ave in Clifton, NJ.

The appointment includes the physical examination, required bloodwork and lab testing, tuberculosis screening, age-based STI testing, and vaccination record review. Dr. Weiss completes the civil surgeon portion of the form, and the office seals the I-693 packet once required results are ready.

What to Bring for Your I-693 Medical Exam

Bring a valid government-issued photo ID, preferably your passport. Bring vaccination records if you have them, even if they are old or in another language. Bring any prior TB test or treatment documentation, a list of medications, and any unsigned copy of Form I-693 that your attorney gave you. Do not sign Form I-693 before the visit.

Timing the I-693 Around Your Filing

Most applicants book once the rest of the filing is close to ready or after USCIS requests the medical exam. The goal is simple: have a current, complete, sealed I-693 that matches the application your attorney or family is preparing.

Why the Civil Surgeon Matters

An I-693 problem can delay an immigration case for administrative reasons even when the medical exam itself is routine. Missing signatures, outdated form versions, incomplete vaccination records, broken seals, or inconsistent dates can create avoidable issues. This office is focused on the immigration medical exam workflow so those details are handled carefully.
