What's Driving the Results

Three things working together — a high-converting website, efficient advertising, and a wider patient reach.

A clear, high-converting website

The website rebuild produced a measurable lift in how effectively visitors become booked patients. The site's click-to-booking conversion rate reached approximately 5% in May — strong for healthcare search advertising, where 2–4% is typical.

Efficient, tightly-targeted advertising

A focused Google Ads campaign reaches patients actively searching for immigration medical exams in the area. On under $1,000 per month in ad spend, the campaign has produced a return of roughly nine dollars in exam revenue for every dollar spent — and that figure is conservative, since it counts only bookings we can directly verify.

A wider patient reach

The website extends the practice's reach well beyond walk-in distance. Online-driven patients travel from a meaningfully wider area than traditional walk-in patients, opening up a catchment of roughly 15 miles around the office.

What Was BuiltThe system is more than a Google Ads campaign — it's an entire brand and search presence built from scratch.

unitedmedicalexams.com

Purpose-built conversion website with Calendly booking integration, trust-building design, and mobile-first layout. 68% of all ad traffic arrives on mobile.

Brand Identity & Call Tracking

The Opportunity Ahead

The practice currently performs roughly 25 immigration exams per month. With Dr. Weiss's capacity of up to 100 exams per month, there is significant room to grow.

Current Capacity Utilization
25 of 100
The advertising campaign is currently limited by budget — meaning qualified patients are searching but not always reached. Methodically increasing investment, in measured steps, is expected to increase booked volume while maintaining a strong return.

The clear next step is a staged increase in advertising investment, measured carefully at each level. With 75% of capacity still available, there is significant room to scale the patient acquisition system while maintaining the same strong unit economics.
