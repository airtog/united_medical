The Snowball Effect

The system didn't just add website bookings on top of existing volume. It created a compounding growth engine that lifted every channel — including ones that have nothing to do with ads. The branding, SEO, and local search presence are doing the heavy lifting.

What Was Built Beyond AdsThe system is more than a Google Ads campaign. It's an entire brand and search presence built from scratch.
unitedmedicalexams.com
Purpose-built conversion website with Calendly booking integration, trust-building design with emotional patient imagery, and mobile-first layout. 68% of all ad traffic arrives on mobile.
15 City-Specific SEO Pages
Individual landing pages targeting immigration medical exam searches in Clifton, Passaic, Paterson, Garfield, Hackensack, Wayne, Paramus, Fort Lee, Elmwood Park, Fair Lawn, Totowa, Little Falls, Woodland Park, West Paterson, and Nutley. Each page is optimized for local search intent — so when a patient in any of these cities searches for an immigration exam, United Medical Exams appears.
Google Business Profile & Maps Optimization
The practice now appears in Google Maps and local pack results for immigration medical exam searches across North Jersey. Patients who find the practice through Maps often call directly — these show up as traditional appointments, not web bookings.
Brand Identity
"United Medical Exams" — a patient-facing brand name, logo, and visual identity built specifically for immigration exam patients.
Call Tracking + AI Agent
Dedicated phone number with Quo call tracking and Sona AI answering agent handling after-hours calls — 179 tracked calls since December.
Traditional Appointments Grew 6.3× — That's the BrandWalk-in and phone bookings climbed steadily even during weeks with $0 ad spend. These patients found the practice through SEO, Google Maps, and word of mouth — all created by the system.
